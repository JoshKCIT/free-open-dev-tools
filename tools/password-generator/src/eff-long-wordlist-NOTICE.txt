This is EFF's "long" wordlist for generating secure, memorable passphrases,
containing 7,776 words (6^5, one per five-die roll), each word worth
log2(7776), approximately 12.9248 bits of entropy on its own.

Bundled here as tools/password-generator/src/eff-long-wordlist.ts, with the
dice-roll number prefix from the original published file stripped; the word
list itself is otherwise unmodified, one word per array entry, in the
original published order. Four entries are compound words carrying an
internal hyphen, exactly as EFF publishes them: drop-down, felt-tip, t-shirt
and yo-yo.
